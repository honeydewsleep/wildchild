Counter Button Wiring

Three momentary buttons on the back of the Cheap Yellow Display. Every button sits between one input pin and GND. The black button's pin has no internal pull-up, so it gets a 10 kΩ resistor to 3V3.

ButtonSignal wireReturn wireExtra
Green (+)CN1 IO22CN1 GNDnone
Red (−)CN1 IO27CN1 GNDnone; hold at power-on for the setup portal
Black (OK)P3 IO35CN1 GND (or P3 GND)10 kΩ from IO35 to CN1 3V3
  1. Plug the two JST pigtails that came with the board into CN1 and P3. Wire colours on the pigtails vary, so identify each lead by its position against the silkscreen label, not by colour.
  2. Solder the resistor between the IO35 lead and the 3V3 lead near the connectors, and heat-shrink it.
  3. Twist each button's pair, keep the runs short, and route them away from the motor leads.
  4. Button polarity does not matter. On buttons with NO, NC and COM terminals use NO and COM.
  5. Leave P3's IO21 unconnected: it drives the screen backlight.
